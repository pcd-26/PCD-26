# Programmazione Concorrente e Distribuita - ISI LM - a.y. 2025/2026

## Repository Purpose

This repository is the working portfolio for the Practical Part of the exam.
It collects the assignment projects developed during the course and serves as
the main reference material for the oral discussion.

## About the Exam

`v1.0.0-20260215`

The exam includes two parts:
1. Practical part
2. Oral discussion

### Practical Part

The practical part concerns the development of assignments proposed during the
course.

- There are 4 assignments, one for each main part of the course.
- Assignments are announced during the course, with no fixed delivery deadlines.
- Assignments can be done individually or in groups (up to 3 students; exceptions must be discussed).
- Assignments must be available by the day before the oral discussion date.
- Assignments are not partial exams: they are portfolio material used to structure the oral discussion.
- Assignments do not expire.

### Oral Discussion

The oral discussion covers both:

- The assignments developed in this repository
- Key concepts and methods discussed during the course

The discussion is individual.

## How to Schedule the Exam

To schedule the exam date, send an email to `a.ricci@unibo.it` indicating the
week in which you would like to take the oral exam. A reply will provide
specific proposed time slots.

There are no fixed dates on AlmaEsami; the date is arranged by email.
